## Field: Acceptance criteria justification ("Expectations" — claim which ones)

We submit this work under Expectations 3 and 4.

**(3) Open a new pathway in an existing research direction, with clear
potential for multi-pronged follow-up work.** The paper introduces an
operational, eigenstate-resolved way to assess weak-ergodicity-breaking
subspaces as quantum codes: encode a logical qubit, monitor, and compute the
reference-qubit coherent information against an energy-matched thermal
ensemble. Beyond settling the scar-vs-thermal question (the scar code loses
under every generic measurement, with hierarchical-bootstrap confidence
intervals and a deficit that grows with system size), it isolates two
separable mechanisms — an algebraic leak through the emergent su(2) order
parameter, and thermal hybridization measured by FSA purity, with a
protection crossover at the purest rungs — and anchors the clean limit in an
exactly solvable spin-1 model. Each mechanism is an immediate follow-up
handle: interventional chaoticity knobs (deformed PXP), other scarred models
and Hilbert-space-fragmented subspaces, non-projective monitoring, and
finite-temperature encodings.

**(4) Provide a novel and synergetic link between different research areas.**
The work connects three literatures that have so far only been juxtaposed:
quantum many-body scars / weak ergodicity breaking, the
eigenstate-thermalization-to-error-correction correspondence, and
measurement-induced phase transitions. The link is quantitative — the same
ETH structure that makes chaotic eigenstates good approximate codes is shown
to be what scar subspaces lack operationally — and it corrects a natural
expectation in the scar literature that athermality should aid information
protection.

General acceptance criteria: the manuscript is self-contained (nine
appendices in place of a supplement), every figure is regenerated by a
one-command open package (doi:10.5281/zenodo.21642056, source at
github.com/deeptell-inc/chaosec), literature claims were verified against
primary sources, and all headline numbers carry confidence intervals.

## Field: Author comments (history disclosure)

This manuscript was considered at PRX Quantum and Physical Review B; both
returned it at the editorial stage on journal-selectivity grounds, in
identical template language and without technical criticism or external
review. Between and after those decisions the paper went through three rounds
of adversarial internal review (documented in the repository's claims
ledger): statistics were hardened to hierarchical-bootstrap confidence
intervals with multi-seed audits, a size-dependence analysis was added (the
local deficit grows from -0.16 to -0.38 for L=10 to 18), and the mechanism
section was rebuilt around the FSA-purity crossover.
