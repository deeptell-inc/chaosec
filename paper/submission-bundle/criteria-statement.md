# PRX Quantum acceptance-criteria statement (submission form, ≤100 words)

**Criteria claimed:** 2 (substantial connection between areas) and 4 (timely,
high-interest topic). Criteria 1/3 deliberately not claimed (a negative result
should not assert "significant advance" / "exceptional importance" — reviewers
penalize overclaiming).

**Statement (91 words):**

This work satisfies criteria 2 and 4. It establishes a substantial connection
between quantum error correction and many-body physics, turning the
correspondence between eigenstate thermalization and approximate error
correction into an operational design principle: protection under monitoring is
set by a code's spread in the measurement basis, quantified by an exact
single-shot predictor. On this basis it refutes the natural expectation
that quantum many-body scars make superior quantum memories. Monitored dynamics
and scars are timely, high-interest topics, and our sign-reversing baseline
artifact is an immediately useful caution for eigenstate-based benchmarking.
